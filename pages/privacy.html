Privacy matters
on every route.

This starter runs most tools in your browser. Review this notice and replace it with a complete policy before collecting personal data or launching publicly.

What this starter stores

The My Trip page and reminders use browser local storage when available. Those entries stay in that browser profile and are not automatically synced to a server.

External links

When you open Google Maps, Skyscanner, IRCTC or another provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Location and SMS

This starter does not track background location and does not send SMS. A future location or messaging feature must explain what is collected, request appropriate permission and consent, protect data, and provide a way to stop notifications.

Before production

Publish a legally reviewed privacy policy, define retention and deletion practices, secure backend services, and comply with applicable privacy, consumer and communications rules.
